## Conclusion

The required clean and positive Paper live paths are verified. Automatic
detection starts after join, clean clients are not kicked, and confirmed
positive evidence invokes the configured Paper kick. P3.6 does not establish
Folia live compatibility or the remaining multi-player/race stress cases.

P4 should remain blocked until those additional live cases are intentionally
run in an isolated environment.
